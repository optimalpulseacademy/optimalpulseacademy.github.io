Who it's for

Many credentialing roles in US healthcare are remote, and most people learn the work on the job by trial and error. This guide gives you the full process in one place.

1

Starting a new career

You want to become a credentialing specialist, including remote and work-from-home roles, and need to understand the whole process before your first job.

2

Billers adding a skill

You already work in billing or RCM and want to handle enrollment and credentialing for your providers with confidence.

3

Practices and agencies

You enroll one provider or many, in one state or several, and want a clear checklist, timeline and tracking system.

About OptimalPulse Academy

OptimalPulse Academy creates training for people who work, or want to work, in US medical billing, revenue cycle management and provider credentialing.

Our material is written by an AAPC-certified medical coder (certified since 2021) with nearly 5 years of hands-on US medical billing experience, who works with US providers every day, across behavioral health, orthopedics, primary care, urgent care and more. Each guide is checked against official sources such as CMS, CAQH and NPPES.

  • Built from real day-to-day work, not theory only
