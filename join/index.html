Here's what the course will teach you, step by step

The CPB billing course opens at the end of December. It starts from zero, so no billing background is needed. We'll email you on launch day.

  1. Modules 1A and 1B · Types of insurance. Medicare, Medicaid, commercial plans, TRICARE and more, and which one pays first.
  2. Module 2 · Reimbursement and collections. Allowed amounts, deductibles, coinsurance, write-offs and patient balances.
  3. Module 3 · Claims and billing. The CMS-1500 and UB-04, clean claims, rejections, denials and corrected claims.
  4. Module 4 · Billing regulations. Timely filing, ABNs, fraud and abuse laws, and the No Surprises Act.
  5. Module 5 · Coding for billers. How CPT, ICD-10-CM, HCPCS and modifiers affect payment.
  6. Module 6 · HIPAA and compliance. Protecting patient information and running a compliant billing office.
  7. Module 7 · Case analysis. Reading real remittances and deciding the next step, like the largest part of the CPB exam.
