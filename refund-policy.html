Exchange & refund policy

How exchanges and refunds work for our digital products and printed books.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to all purchases from OptimalPulse Academy, including digital products (PDF guides, toolkits and templates) and printed books delivered in Egypt.

1. Digital products (PDF guide, toolkit, templates)

Digital products are delivered instantly by email or download link and can be copied once delivered, so digital sales are final and cannot be exchanged or returned, except in these cases:

  • Not received: you paid but did not receive the download link within 24 hours, and we cannot resend it.
  • Duplicate purchase: you were charged twice for the same product.
  • Faulty file: the file is damaged or will not open, and we cannot send you a working copy within 3 business days.

In these cases you receive a full refund. Please contact us within 14 days of purchase.

2. Printed books (delivery inside Egypt)

  • Damaged, misprinted or wrong item: contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a free replacement or give a full refund, including delivery, as you prefer.
  • Change of mind: you may return an unused book in its original condition within 14 days of delivery. We refund the price of the book; delivery and return shipping costs are paid by the customer.
  • Exchange: an unused printed book can be exchanged for the PDF edition within 14 days of delivery, and we refund the price difference.

3. How to request a refund or exchange

Email info@optimalpulseacademy.com or message us on WhatsApp at +20 101 555 0417 with your name, order date, the email or phone used for payment, and the reason. We reply within 2 business days.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the same payment method used for the purchase (card, wallet or bank transfer) within 14 days of approval. Your bank or wallet provider may take a few extra days to show the refund.

5. Purchases on other platforms

Purchases made on Amazon (Kindle and paperback), Gumroad or Payhip are also subject to that platform's own refund rules.
